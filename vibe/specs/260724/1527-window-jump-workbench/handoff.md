Confirm list-pinned rows lead and every other row stays application-sorted. Confirm assigning a live row to a stable slot does not create a favorite. For WJ-08, clear diagnostics, verify an assigned valid slot activates with no blocking record, then close its target and verify the only non-success record is accepted `target-closed` after a rescan.
